Research

Recent independent work

Measured · pre-registered

SIHRE: governing a committee of signals

A framework I originated for trusting different reasoning methods by their measured reliability. Its first out-of-sample test, 1975–2026, is net of costs: trust lifted the Sharpe ratio from −0.02 to 0.30 against equal weighting (95% CI of the gain [0.12, 0.58]). The abstention component was falsified, and the page says so.

 Research · design candidate

CELL//OS: a governed operating system for AI-agent work

The product vision and research programme. It shows how teams of AI agents could run under human authority, with replayable evidence. Every capability is labelled Implemented, Demonstrated, Designed or Research, with interactive demos on fictional data. The map shows how the pieces fit.

How I work

Evidence before claims

Decide what would count, first

Pass criteria, holdouts and stop rules are fixed before the run, and hashed where it matters, so the result can't shape the test.

Prove the instrument can see

A check must fail on a known-bad case before its pass means anything. A zero from a blind instrument is never reported as a zero.

Report the failures

Falsified ideas and flawed tests stay in the record. They are the most useful part of it.
