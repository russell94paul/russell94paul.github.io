How it fits together

CELL//OS is one platform. Products apply it to a single field; demos let you try a product on fictional data; solutions are ways to put it to work; research mechanisms are proposals for how its agents should reason. This page sorts every named piece into one of those five kinds and shows how each connects to the platform.

The map
Two labels, two claims. A product and its demo carry separate labels. CELL-Q the product is a concept; its lab prototype runs today, so the lab is Demonstrated. Where another page gives a single label, this map splits them.
