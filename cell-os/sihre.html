SIHRE: governing a committee of reasoning methods

Self-Improving Heterogeneous Reasoning Ensemble. SIHRE is a framework for running several different reasoning methods together. A meta-orchestrator decides which method to trust on a given problem, lowers its trust in a method whose calibration is slipping, escalates to verification when trusted methods disagree, and abstains rather than force a weak conclusion. In CELL//OS it is the proposed way an Operative thinks.

Origin

Trading research

It began as a 13-signal architecture for my own futures-trading research. It fused statistical, causal, knowledge-graph, uncertainty and regime-detection experts at the reasoning level rather than averaging predictions. I then generalised it to any domain.

What is new

The combination, not the parts

No single mechanism is new: expert advice, mixture-of-experts and conformal prediction all exist. The candidate contribution is governing trust, verification, abstention and memory together, as one adaptive system.

Status

Research, now with one test

The trust mechanism has passed one pre-registered out-of-sample test. The abstention mechanism failed the same test. The full architecture is not implemented inside CELL//OS.

In plain terms

Five advisers, one of them usually wrong

Picture a committee of five advisers. Some are usually right. One, as it turns out, is usually wrong. And who is right changes over the years. Averaging everyone equally gives the bad adviser a full vote. SIHRE keeps a running record of how each adviser's recent calls actually turned out, and gives more weight to whoever has been reliable lately, without anyone hand-picking the winner in advance. Where trusted advisers contradict each other, it can decline to act or ask for more evidence.

First empirical testMeasured · one domainTrading-domain research · CELL-TRADER honesty bar

Does trust beat a plain average, on data the design never saw?

I tested two SIHRE mechanisms on five classic equity signals: momentum, short-term reversal, long-term reversal, low volatility and 52-week high. The data are the Ken French 49 US industry portfolios, monthly, 1926–2026: public, academically standard and free of survivorship bias.

  • Trust: each signal is weighted by its recent information coefficient (how well its ranking predicted the next month).
  • Abstention: positions are taken only where trusted signals agree.

Pre-registered. The data were cut off at 1974 before any computation. 24 configurations were compared on 1931–1974 only. The chosen settings and four hypotheses were written down and SHA-256-hashed, and the 1975–2026 holdout (620 months) was then run once. All figures are net of 10 bps per unit of turnover.

What worked: not trusting a bad signal

One-month reversal works between individual stocks but loses money between industries (Moskowitz & Grinblatt, 1999). Trust learned this unaided and cut its average weight from 20% to about 9%. Adaptive trust matched weights fixed from 1931–1974 (0.30 vs 0.31). Its advantage is needing no long calibration history, and noticing when a signal decays.

What failed: abstention, as specified

Dropping industries where signals disagreed concentrated and churned the book: turnover rose from 0.82 to 1.43 a month. At zero cost it would have earned 0.35; at 25 bps, 0.05. The idea that disagreement should lower conviction stands; this implementation of it does not. Next: scale total exposure by agreement, with a turnover penalty.

Limits: industry portfolios, not individual stocks; a flat cost model; one dataset; no combination protected against the 2009 momentum crash, where every blend lost 28–34%. A backtest is not evidence of future return. No capital was deployed.

Where this applies

Any place several models answer the same question

Quant investing

Blend alpha signals so that one whose predictive power has decayed loses weight automatically, without waiting for a quarterly review; cut exposure where signals conflict. (This study.)

Retail demand forecasting

Lean on whichever forecast model has recently been accurate for each product and region; send forecasts where models disagree sharply to a human planner instead of auto-ordering.

Fraud and credit risk

Trust each scorer by its recent precision; route the cases where models disagree to manual review, the costly but correct form of abstaining.

AI and agent systems

Route each task to the model that has been reliable on that kind of task; escalate to verification or a person when models disagree. This is SIHRE's role inside CELL//OS.

What it would takeResearch

The evidence still missing

  • Stock-level replication, with point-in-time constituents and a richer cost model.
  • Abstention v2, pre-registered before its own holdout: exposure scaled by agreement, with a turnover penalty.
  • Faster trust under stress: a short-memory component or regime detector for crash months like 2009.
  • Calibration and failure-correlation analysis, which tests whether the chosen experts fail independently or together.
  • A domain outside finance, to test the domain-agnostic claim rather than assert it.
